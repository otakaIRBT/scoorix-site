Privacy

Last updated October 20, 2026.

This policy covers scoorix.ai, the website of ScooriX Inc. Our apps, such as Miletus, have their own privacy policies, which explain what each app keeps and why.

What this website collects

Nothing about you. The site sets no cookies, uses no analytics or advertising tools, and loads nothing from other companies. A link to a customer's website takes you to their site, under their own privacy policy. Like any website, the service that hosts it may keep short-lived technical logs (such as an address and the time of a request) to keep the site running and safe.

When you email us

If you write to us (for example solutions@scoorix.ai or contact@scoorix.ai), we keep your message and your email address to reply and to follow up on what you asked about. We don't sell or share them, and we'll delete them if you ask.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with its date.
